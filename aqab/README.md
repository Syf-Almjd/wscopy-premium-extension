## 🚀 How to Install in Google Chrome (10 Seconds)

1. Open **Google Chrome** (or Brave, Edge, Arc, Opera).
2. Go to the Extensions management page:
   - Type `chrome://extensions` in the address bar and press Enter.
3. In the top-right corner, toggle **Developer mode** to **ON**.
4. In the top-left corner, click **Load unpacked**.
5. Select this directory:
   ```
   /Users/saifalmajd/saif/wa-copy/aqab
   ```
6. **Done!** You'll see `WACopy Pro - WhatsApp Chat Exporter & AI Copy` active with its emerald icon.

## 💬 How to Use on WhatsApp Web

1. Open **[WhatsApp Web](https://web.whatsapp.com)** in your browser and log in with your phone QR code.
2. Click on any contact or group chat you want to export.
3. You will see:
   - A green **`[ ⚡ WACopy ]`** button directly in the chat header bar next to the search icon.
   - A floating **`⚡ WACopy PRO`** badge in the bottom-right corner.
   - Or click the **WACopy** icon in your Chrome toolbar.
4. Click the button to open the **Export Studio**:
   - **One-Click AI Copy**: Copies the chat formatted for ChatGPT / Claude with 1 click.
   - **Range Options**: Visible only (instant), 100, 500, 2000, Entire History (auto-scrolls all messages), or Date Range.
   - **AI Prompt Templates**:
     - *No prompt (Raw chat)*
     - *Summarize discussion & next steps*
     - *Extract action items & assignees*
     - *Convert to formal meeting minutes*
     - *Key questions, answers & resources*
     - *Custom Prompt*
   - **Export Formats**:
     - **Markdown (`.md`)**: Best for pasting into LLMs, Notion, Obsidian.
     - **HTML (`.html`)**: Beautiful standalone offline viewer with built-in search, sender filters, dark/light theme toggle, and PDF print.
     - **PDF (`.pdf`)**: Formatted chat bubbles ready for clean document printing.
     - **Plain Text (`.txt`)**: Clean text log compatible with standard WhatsApp export.
     - **CSV (`.csv`)**: Excel / Google Sheets compatible spreadsheet.
     - **JSON (`.json`)**: Raw structured developer data with full metadata.
